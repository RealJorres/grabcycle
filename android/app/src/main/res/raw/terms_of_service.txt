1. The service
GrabCycle connects passengers with independent, accredited tricycle drivers in its configured service area in Marinduque. [Describe the operator's legal relationship with drivers.]

2. Accounts
• One account per person, registered with a verified Philippine mobile number. You must provide accurate information and keep your account secure.
• Passengers must pass identity verification before booking. Drivers must be accredited (license, vehicle registration, franchise/permit and other documents required by the operator) before going online.
• We may suspend accounts for fraud, safety concerns, expired documents or violations of these terms. Suspensions apply across devices.

3. Bookings and fares
• Fares are calculated by GrabCycle from the published fare rules shown before you confirm. Where road distance is unavailable, the estimate is clearly labelled.
• The initial payment method is cash paid to the driver at the end of the trip. Discrepancies can be reported in the app.
• Waiting fees, discounts, cancellation fees or other charges apply only if published in the fare rules and shown to you in advance.

4. Conduct and safety
• Treat each other with respect. Do not request or offer rides outside the app to avoid platform safeguards.
• Drivers must comply with traffic laws and must not use the app while driving.
• In an emergency, call 911. GrabCycle's in-app emergency button opens your phone dialer and lets you message your emergency contact; it does not automatically alert GrabCycle or emergency services.

5. Ratings and reports
Both parties may rate each other after a completed trip. Reports are reviewed by GrabCycle support.
